# SAAS Engineering Technical Services Ltd

Official public corporate website for **SAAS Engineering Technical Services Ltd**, an engineering contractor based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.

## Core Divisions & Industry Capabilities

1. **Welding & Fabrication, Industrial Services & Training**
   - Structural steel fabrication, automated oxy-fuel track torch cutting, Pinacho CNC lathe machining, hydrostatic test rigs, and certified pipefitting training.
2. **Oil & Gas Services, Surface Pipeline & Offshore Support**
   - Surface pipeline trenching and laying, field pipe welding, pipe profile saddle beveling, and offshore platform support.
3. **Dredging Services & Technical Parts (Valves, Impellers, Dredging Pumps)**
   - Diesel engine dredge pump skids, cast slurry impellers, machined volute pump casings, DN200 8-inch pumps, industrial valves, and METRUS testing.
4. **Logistics, Haulage, Heavy Equipment Leasing & Caterpillar Parts**
   - Caterpillar sideboom pipelayers, heavy hydraulic excavators, multi-axle heavy transport trailers, and genuine Caterpillar replacement spares.
5. **Manpower Supply, Instrumentation & Control Engineering**
   - Certified technical personnel staffing, instrumentation calibration test benches, pressure transmitters, and SCADA automation control systems.
6. **General Contracts, Procurement & Industrial Safety Gadgets (PPE)**
   - Certified personal protective equipment (safety helmets, face shields, fall-arrest harnesses), warehouse materials procurement, and general contracting.
